FAQ

Common Questions

How do you ensure product quality?

Every order includes pre-shipment inspection with photos and a checklist. For critical batches we can arrange 1-by-1 checks and third-party testing such as SGS, with reports provided before shipment.

Are your prices competitive?

We quote directly from the factory floor and encourage you to compare. Our service fee is transparent — no hidden markups, no kickbacks from suppliers.

Do you support small orders or mixed cartons?

Yes. You can start with a small trial order, and we combine different products into one carton or one container to keep your freight costs low.

How long does delivery take?

Sample lead time is usually 7–15 days and production 20–40 days depending on the product. We plan shipping with you and track every step.
