FAQ

Questions Buyers Ask Before Ordering

What is your MOQ?

Usually 3000 sqm per color for stock specifications. Custom colors and special treatments may require a larger MOQ.

Can I get a free sample?

Yes. A4 samples are free for qualified buyers; courier cost is on the buyer and refundable on first order.

What is the lead time?

15–20 days after deposit for regular orders. Loading photos and QC data are provided before shipment.

Do you provide test reports?

Yes. We provide weight, tensile, tear, adhesion and cold-crack data. REACH, RoHS and flame-retardant reports are available on request.
